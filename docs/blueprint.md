# **App Name**: AuraFlow Wellness

## Core Features:

- Client Booking & AI Recommendation: Clients can browse the full service catalog, utilize an AI tool to recommend personalized massage services based on their input, and book appointments securely. Immediate email confirmations are sent upon reservation.
- Client Account Management: Clients securely log in via a passwordless magic link to manage their personal details, view a chronological timeline of upcoming and past appointments, manage cancellations, and download Swiss-compliant invoices.
- Therapist Practice Dashboard: A secure therapist-only dashboard providing a 'Daily Pulse' view with a vertical timeline of appointments, client consultation reasons, financial summaries, and a Cointrin local weather widget.
- Integrated Client CRM: Allows the therapist to search for clients, view and edit their comprehensive profiles, including contact details, insurance information, consultation history, and associated appointments and invoices.
- Swiss Invoicing & Revenue Management: Enables the therapist to generate Swiss-compliant invoices with therapist RCC numbers, manage payment statuses (Paid/Pending), allow clients to download PDF invoices, and export revenue data for accounting.
- AI Aftercare Tool: After a session, an AI tool generates personalized aftercare tips and wellness advice for clients based on the booked service and client notes, delivered via email and displayed in the client portal.
- Availability & Notification System: The therapist can easily block specific dates/times and set recurring availability via a calendar, dynamically updating client booking options. Automated reminder emails are sent 24 hours prior to appointments.

## Style Guidelines:

- Background color: A warm off-white cream (#F7F7F2), providing a clean, bright canvas.
- Primary accent color: A rich, deep forest green (#196640), evoking serenity and natural elegance, for key UI elements and branding.
- Secondary accent color: A vibrant warm gold (#E3BD28), used sparingly for highlights, calls-to-action, and to convey luxury.
- Headings and prominent text: 'Alegreya' (serif), lending an elegant, intellectual, and trustworthy feel suitable for a premium wellness brand.
- Body text and UI elements: 'Inter' (sans-serif), ensuring readability, cleanliness, and a modern aesthetic across the application.
- Elegant, minimalistic icons that align with a premium Swiss wellness aesthetic, featuring clean lines and a sophisticated touch.
- A high-contrast, spacious layout with generous whitespace. UI elements will feature soft shadows and subtly rounded cards to prevent clutter and maintain a luxurious feel.
- Subtle and refined micro-interactions, providing gentle feedback and enhancing the user experience without being distracting.
